About DeviX Academy

Empowering People Through Technology and Innovation.

Founded in Indore with technical mentorship led by Ramgopal Prajapati (Ram Sir / @iamramsir), DeviX Academy helps college students (B.Tech, M.Tech, BCA, MCA, BSc, MSc), aspiring coders, software developers, working professionals, and corporate teams bridge the gap between textbook theory and real-world engineering.

Through structured mentorship, live coding sessions, AI automation workflows, and hands-on capstone builds, learners gain tangible capability in Python, Data Analytics, Data Science, Generative AI, Full Stack Engineering, Cloud, and Cyber Security.

Our Mission

Deliver accessible, practical technology & AI education built around real-world software, analytics, and problem solving.

Our Vision

Build confident builders, innovators, and future-ready technology leaders across campuses and enterprises.

Training Philosophy

Learn by building: live demonstrations, guided debugging, real datasets, and evaluated portfolio milestones.

Industry Curriculum

Continuously refreshed modules aligned with modern AI tools, full-stack APIs, BI dashboards, and cloud standards.
